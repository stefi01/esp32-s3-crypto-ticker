Desk Coin — 4.0 inch Hosyond / LCD wiki E32R40T

Arduino IDE
  Board: ESP32 Dev Module
  Flash size: 4MB
  Partition scheme: Huge APP (3MB No OTA)
  Upload speed: 921600
  Port: the COM port for this screen

Open this sketch:
  firmware\4 inch version\desk_coin\desk_coin.ino

Do not flash this file onto the 7 inch.
Do not replace the train tracker or the plane tracker with this sketch.

Screen: rotation 3, SD slot on top.
Touch uses the same corner calibration already stored on this board.
Wi-Fi, the coin, the chart, the time zone, and brightness are stored on their own.
Factory on the Wi-Fi page clears only this desk, not the train or plane settings.
